How to Play

  1. 1. Pick a level and start a round of 10 words. You have 3 lives and 45 seconds per word.
  2. 2. Press Listen to hear the word (or Flash Word to see it for 2 seconds), then type its spelling.
  3. 3. A wrong answer, a skip, or running out of time costs 1 life. Hints halve the points for that word.
  4. 4. Consecutive correct answers build a streak bonus. Beat your best score!
